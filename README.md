# 🌱 BioCraft Network

**BioCraft Network** is a Minecraft server network focused on fun, creativity, and community. With over 10 years of on-and-off history, BioCraft is returning — refreshed, reimagined, and ready to grow.

We’re putting players first, building a place where both long-time fans and new adventurers feel welcome. No pay-to-win gimmicks. No pressure. Just pure Minecraft, powered by passion and community.

---

## 🧭 Our Philosophy

- **Community Over Monetization:** We're done chasing paywalls and perks. BioCraft’s future is all about a chill, engaging experience that evolves with its players.
- **Cross-Generational Play:** Whether you’re a returning veteran or a brand new explorer, there’s a place for you here.
